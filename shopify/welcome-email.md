# "You're on the list" email (Shopify Email automation)

Sent automatically to everyone who signs up: the "Get notified" pop-up and the footer
sign-up. There is no discount code in it. (The old version of this email handed out
WELCOME15; if that automation is still on, turn it off or replace it with this one, or
people will get a code the site no longer mentions.)

## Set it up in Shopify

1. Shopify admin > **Marketing** > **Automations**. If a **Welcome new subscribers**
   automation exists, open it and edit it (or turn it off and create a new one).
2. Open the email step and paste the subject, preview text, and body below. Remove any
   discount block.
3. Point the button to `/collections/kits`.
4. Click **Turn on automation**.

## Subject

You're on the list.

## Preview text

We'll email you the moment kits are in stock.

## Body

**You're on the list.**

Thanks for signing up. We keep fishing simple: pick a kit, bring your own rod and
reel, and go.

Kits aren't in stock yet. When they are, you'll hear from us first. Nothing to pay and
nothing more to do.

Want a head start? Our two-minute guide covers the basics: **[New to Fishing →]**
(`/pages/new-to-fishing`)

Questions? Just reply to this email. We read every one.

Ketto Outdoors
